somebody credible has, and said so where we can read it. *argued* — a chain of
reasoning with no data behind it. Agree these once, as a team, and do not soften
them later.

## Never

Do not add a total column. The exchange rate between a euro, a week and a basis
point of conversion is the strategy, and it changes by quarter — a formula hides
that judgement rather than removing it. See `../scoring.md`.
