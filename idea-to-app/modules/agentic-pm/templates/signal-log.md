# Signal log

Raised by agents, continuously. **A signal is not a backlog item.** It becomes one
only by surviving step 2 with its source attached.

| Date | Agent | Signal | Source | Half-life | Recurred |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [date] | [market / customer / portfolio / engineering / analytics / metrics] | [one sentence] | [the ticket, the call, the dashboard, the competitor's page] | [weeks before it stops counting as evidence] | [dates it came back] |

**Rules.** No source, not a signal. Past its half-life and never recurred, it
drops off. An agent may raise a signal; only step 2 creates a backlog item.
