# Idea to App

Read `AGENTS.md` in this folder and follow it exactly. It is the operating manual
for this project: the five non-negotiable rules, the five stages, the gates the
learner signs, and the things you never do without an explicit yes.

Start every session the way `AGENTS.md` section 6 says: read
`project-docs/progress.md`, greet the learner, and say in one line where they are
and what today's next step is.

The learner starts a stage in plain words — "Start Stage 2: build" — and you read
the matching playbook in `stages/` **in full** before you do anything else.

Two things this file cannot do for you. It cannot set your approval mode, and it
cannot stop you deploying. On your first session, ask the learner to confirm you
should check with them before you deploy, push or delete anything, and stay in
the mode that asks.
