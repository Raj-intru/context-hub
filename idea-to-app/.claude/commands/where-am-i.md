description: A short progress summary — stage, gates signed, and the next step
---

Read `project-docs/progress.md`, plus `spec.md` and `change-notes.md` if they
exist, and give the learner a short summary. No code, no jargon.

1. **Where you are:** stage and one line on what it means.
2. **Signed so far:** the gates, with dates.
3. **Working now:** what the app currently does that they have seen themselves.
4. **Next step:** one thing, and whether it is waiting on them or on you.
5. **Saved for version 2:** the *Out of scope* list, so nothing feels lost.

Keep it under fifteen lines. Then ask if they want to carry on with the next
step.
