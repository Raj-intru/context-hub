Welcome the learner to Idea to App.

1. Read `AGENTS.md` sections 1–7 so you follow the rules exactly.
2. If `project-docs/progress.md` exists, this is not a new learner: run the
   same steps as `/where-am-i` instead.
3. Otherwise, in three short paragraphs, tell the learner:
   - What will happen over four weeks (idea check, spec, build, trust, ship)
   - The deal: you write the code, they make every decision, and nothing moves
     forward without their yes
   - What they will leave with: a live app, the code in their own account, and a
     signed Trust Card
4. Ask for their name and their idea in a sentence or two, then continue with
   `stages/0-idea-check.md`.
